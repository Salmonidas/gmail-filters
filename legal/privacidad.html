2. Data We Collect and Purpose

Gmail Filter Builder is a tool designed under the "Privacy by Design" principle. By default, the application runs locally in your browser (client-side) and does not collect, store, or transmit to our servers any information about your emails, searches, generated filters, or personal usage data.

Data is processed only in the following explicit cases:

  • Technical connection data: When visiting the website, our infrastructure provider (GitHub Pages) may temporarily log your IP address and browser User-Agent for security purposes, DDoS attack prevention, and network performance.
  • Donations / Payments: If you choose to make a voluntary contribution to support the project via the donation system, the payment will be processed entirely by third-party platforms (Lemon Squeezy or GitHub Sponsors). We do not collect or have access to your bank or credit card data at any time.

4. AI Technologies (AI Act)

We explicitly inform you that this website DOES NOT use generative Artificial Intelligence engines or automated decision-making systems in its filter creation functions. All generated code is based on deterministic programming logic.
